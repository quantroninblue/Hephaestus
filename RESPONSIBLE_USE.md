simulation, research, prototyping, testing, and debugging.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their prompts, source files, meshes,
textures, generated outputs, exports, demonstrations, publications, and uses do

A prompt referring to a well-known robot, character, product, logo, vehicle, or
brand does not grant permission to reproduce, distribute, market, or sell that
material.

Users should prefer original designs and should obtain appropriate permission
before using protected names, logos, artwork, meshes, trade dress, characters,
patents, or proprietary engineering data.

The official project should not ship copyrighted third-party meshes, branded
assets, or exact replicas unless their redistribution rights have been
verified. Hephaestus AI is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of
third-party characters, brands, products, or simulation assets.

## Generated output

AI-generated output may resemble existing material, contain errors, or include
content with unclear provenance. Users must review generated output before
publishing, distributing, manufacturing, or commercializing it.

## Harmful and unlawful activity

Do not use Hephaestus to facilitate unlawful activity, bypass safety controls,
violate privacy, infringe intellectual property, or design or operate systems
intended to injure people or damage property.
