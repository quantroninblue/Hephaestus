services. Those components are not relicensed by the Hephaestus project and
remain subject to their own licenses, notices, acceptable-use policies, privacy
policies, and service terms.

Important integrations include:

- ROS 2: https://www.ros.org/
- Gazebo: https://gazebosim.org/
- CadQuery: https://cadquery.readthedocs.io/
- Open CASCADE Technology: https://dev.opencascade.org/
- GroqCloud and the Groq API: https://groq.com/
- GPT-OSS model deployments selected through the configured provider
- Python packages declared in `pyproject.toml` and installed into the user's
  environment

endorsement.

Where a packaged distribution includes third-party code or data, its
redistributor must add the notices required by those bundled components. This
file is an integration overview and is not a substitute for the license files
of redistributed dependencies.
